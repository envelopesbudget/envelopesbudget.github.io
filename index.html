    04 / How to start

    First steps.

    About five minutes. You can skip any step.

    01

    Currency and name

    We filled in the currency from your country — check it. But give the budget a name of your own: that makes it easy to recognize on any of your devices.

    02

    Where your money is

    Add every place your money is kept: cash in your wallet and cards at every bank — with today’s balance. Skip even one, and the budget won’t match what you actually have.

    03

    Assign your money

    Decide how much of this money goes to each category this month: tap the amount next to a category. Start with the essentials — the rest can be assigned later on the «Budget» screen.

    It starts with your phone

    All features.

    Record a purchase at the checkout. Prepare for a big expense. Know what your money can cover. Envelopes connects everyday decisions with your plan.

    01Give your money a jobMake room for what you need today and what matters next.+
    • Your envelopes

      Create categories and groups for bills, daily life and savings. Each shows its plan, spending and balance.

    • A head start on planning

      Assign money using goals, last month’s plan or spending, six-month averages, or percentage shares.

    • Room for change

      Move money between categories, cover overspending and carry your plan into the next month.

    02Prepare for bigger expensesA holiday, insurance or an emergency fund can each have a different savings plan.+
    • By a chosen date

      Set an amount and target month. The app calculates what you still need to set aside using your savings and time remaining.

    • Regular saving or a reserve

      Choose a monthly amount, a balance to maintain, or a percentage of the month’s income.

    • List goal

      List what you need to buy or do in a category this month and enter the amounts — their total becomes the goal. Record an expense from a list item, or move purchases you have not yet recorded to another month.

    • Progress you can see

      Track category funding and use auto-assignment to top up underfunded goals.

    03Record life as it happensOne receipt can belong to several envelopes. Moving money between accounts has its own place.+
    • Purchases and income

      Record the amount, date, account, category and payee. Notes keep the details you might otherwise forget.

    • One receipt, several jobs

      Split a purchase into parts: groceries in one envelope, household supplies in another.

    • An organised history

      Use flags and tags to mark transactions. Day totals, search and filters help you check and find them.

    04Keep up with purchases on the goWhen there is no time to fill everything in, save the essentials and return to the details later.+
    • Inbox

      Enable inbox mode to save an amount without completing the account and category. Unsorted entries stay in the queue.

    • Voice entry

      Dictate a purchase, then check the recognised fields before saving.

    • Receipts with your records

      Attach photos and PDFs to transactions so the supporting details stay close at hand.

    05Keep recurring payments in viewRent, subscriptions and other regular payments deserve a place in your plan ahead of time.+
    • Scheduled transactions

      Create future payments and income, and set a repeat schedule for recurring entries.

    • Coming up next

      See upcoming payments in Overview and review due transactions in a separate list.

    • Reminders

      Set notifications to help you return to your budget and planned payments on time.

    06Bring an event’s spending togetherA trip includes tickets, meals and shopping. A tag brings them together across categories and months.+
    • The whole story

      Tag transactions to see related income, expenses and statistics by category and month.

    • Settlements

      Mark a tag as a settlement. The difference between money spent and received shows who owes whom; a zero balance closes the settlement.

    • What matters now

      The Tags now overview block keeps current events and open settlements in view.

    07See money across your accountsCash, cards, loans and savings each play a part in the bigger picture.+
    • Inside and outside the budget

      Include cash, bank accounts and credit cards in your budget. Track loans and other accounts outside it.

    • Check your balances

      Record transfers between accounts and reconcile balances with the actual amounts.

    • Other currencies

      Your budget uses its chosen currency. Off-budget accounts support separate currencies and greater precision; cross-currency transfers let you enter an exchange rate or the amount received.

    08Build your own home screenGoals might come first for you; upcoming bills might matter more to someone else.+
    • Twelve blocks

      Reorder or hide recent transactions, goals, net worth, large expenses, upcoming events and other overview blocks.

    • A spending calendar

      See the days when you spent more and the days when you spent nothing.

    • Pay yourself

      Track how much of your income you plan to assign to your chosen savings categories.

    09Understand where your money goesLook at this month’s habits or see how things change over a longer period.+
    • Spending in detail

      Compare categories and payees, find larger expenses and follow spending trends.

    • Income and expenses

      Explore income and spending in tables and charts. Choose a period and narrow the view with filters.

    • Beyond one month

      Follow net worth, credit card debt and age of money: the time between receiving money and spending it.

    • Colors you can distinguish

      With enhanced colors enabled, reports use a dedicated palette for light and dark themes. The colors account for red–green color vision differences, making it easier to match the chart to its legend.

    10Make it comfortable for youFamiliar layouts, readable numbers and a look you enjoy are all part of feeling at home.+
    • Appearance

      Combine seven styles with light or dark mode, and choose corners, icons, fonts and flag shapes.

    • Meaningful color

      Color income and expenses, income only, or keep amounts neutral. Flag-based row tinting can be switched off.

    • When colors are hard to tell apart

      Enable enhanced colors to show income in blue instead of green, making it easier to distinguish from red expenses. This setting works in both light and dark themes.

    • Reading preferences

      Adjust density, number and date formats, and the first day of the week.

    A few good questions.

    Do I need to register?+

    No. Create a budget on your device and start recording. An external storage account is only needed if you choose to connect cloud synchronisation.

    Can I use it offline?+

    Yes. You can manage your budget, record transactions and view reports without internet access. A connection is only needed for synchronisation and backups through cloud storage.

    Do I need a computer?+

    No. Everyday tracking, planning, goals and reports are available on Android. The Windows app is an optional companion for people who prefer working at a computer.

    Where do I start if I have never kept a budget?+

    Add your accounts and their current balances, create a few categories and assign the money you already have. You do not have to reconstruct your entire spending history. The built-in setup guide helps with the first steps.

    Can I budget with an irregular income?+

    The plan starts with money you already have. When more income arrives, you can assign it to current expenses and future priorities. You do not need a fixed salary or a particular payday.

    What if a category runs out of money?+

    Move money from another category and adjust your plan. Auto-assignment can also cover overspending. Changing the plan does not remove purchases you have already recorded.

    Can I split one receipt across categories?+

    Yes. Split a transaction into parts and give each a category. Groceries and household supplies can stay on one receipt while being recorded against different envelopes.

    Can I track credit cards and loans?+

    Yes. Credit cards have a dedicated budget account type. Loans can be tracked in off-budget accounts. This lets you keep them alongside cash and bank accounts while preserving their different roles.

    Can I save towards a specific date?+

    Yes. Set an amount and target month, and the app calculates what you still need to set aside. You can also choose monthly saving, a balance to maintain, a percentage of income, or a List goal based on the total of planned purchases in a category for the month.

    How do I move my budget to another phone?+

    Restore a backup or sync through your own Google Drive, Dropbox or WebDAV storage. Devices on the same local network can also transfer a budget over Wi-Fi.

    Will my cloud provider be able to read my budget?+

    Enable cloud encryption and set a password: the sync file, backups and receipt photos will be encrypted on your device before uploading. The provider receives encrypted files instead of readable budget data. Your password is sent neither to the cloud nor to the developer; use the same password on your other devices. Keep it somewhere safe — we cannot recover a forgotten password. This protection is optional and does not encrypt local files on your device.

    Does synchronisation replace a backup?+

    No. Synchronisation helps transfer changes between devices, while a backup keeps a separate budget state that you can restore. The app supports both.

    Can I protect access to my budget?+

    Yes. Set up a PIN to enter the app. Biometric unlocking is also available on supported devices.

    Are there settings for people with color vision deficiencies?+

    Yes. Enhanced colors account for protanopia and deuteranopia, which affect red–green color discrimination. Income appears in blue instead of green, and reports use a dedicated palette with more distinguishable colors. The setting works in light and dark themes.

    Can I move from YNAB?+

    Yes. Import a YNAB 4 backup or web YNAB export files. Check accounts, transactions and balances after importing. This is a file import, not ongoing synchronisation with YNAB.

    Can I export my transactions?+

    Yes. Export the current budget’s transactions to CSV to open them in a spreadsheet or keep a separate copy. Use a budget backup when you need to restore the budget.

    How much does it cost?+

    The Windows version is free to install. Check Google Play for the current Android price and in-app purchase terms in your region.
