What the app can do

“My Budget” isn't a spending log — it's a full budget built on the envelope method. It can do a lot, and most of it isn't buried deep in menus but hidden in gestures and small touches. This page is a quick tour; after that, each screen gets its own article.

The essentials

Expense tracking answers the question “where did the money go”. A budget answers a more important one: “what can it still cover” — today, until payday, before your vacation.

Envelopes

Every dollar gets a job: groceries, rent, vacation. You see how much is left in each envelope, and overspending turns red right away. More in “How the budget works”.

Your data is yours

Your budget lives on your phone and in your cloud. The developer has no access to it. You can lock the cloud with a password — AES-256. More in “Cloud and backups”.

Moving from YNAB

Accounts, categories and your whole history from YNAB 4 or web YNAB move over as a file, and a small glossary shows what familiar terms are called here. More in “First steps”.

Quick entry

It's easier to record a purchase at the checkout than to remember it in the evening. That's why there are several ways to do it.

To the inbox

No time to pick a category and account at the checkout? Just enter the amount. The “To inbox” button saves it marked “Sort”, and at home the form opens these transactions one by one. Turn it on in “Settings → Data → Inbox”. More in “Transaction form”.

By voice

“Whole Foods 70 groceries from account Checking” — and the form is filled in. More in “Voice input”.

From a receipt

Point the camera at the receipt's QR code — the amount and date fill in by themselves, and from the second time on the app recognizes the store by its register. Works where the app can read your country's receipts. More in “Receipt scanner”.

From the home screen

Widgets and shortcuts open the form with one tap, without going into the app. More in “Widgets and shortcuts”.

Calculator keypad

Amounts are typed on the app's own keypad with addition and subtraction, split across several categories, transferred between accounts — even in different currencies. More in “Transaction form”.

Plans

Goals

Five kinds: monthly, by a date, keep a balance, percent of income, and a shopping list. The app works out how much to set aside. More in “Goals”.

Scheduled payments

Rent, subscriptions, loan payments — with a reminder ahead of time and entered in one tap. More in “Scheduled payments”.

Credit cards and debt

Money for paying off a credit card is set aside automatically, and you see the debt month by month. More in the scenario “Credit cards and debt”.

Tags and “who owes whom”

A trip, a renovation, shared costs with friends — a tag gathers them from different categories and works out who owes whom. More in “Payees and tags”.

Reports

Ten reports: where the money went, trend and deviation from usual, money flow from income to spending, income and expenses by month, age of money, net worth, credit card debt. And “Overview” is your home screen: you pick the blocks and arrange them yourself. More in “Reports” and “Overview”.

Devices and cloud

One budget on your phone, tablet and Windows computer — for two or for the whole family. Sync through Google Drive, Dropbox or WebDAV — Nextcloud, ownCloud, pCloud, your own server; backups are made automatically on a schedule. More in the scenarios “One budget on several devices” and “Two or more people, one budget”.

Make it yours

Thirteen languages, sixty currencies, amount and date formats for your country. Seven styles in light and dark, fonts, density, corner shapes, icons. Several budgets: personal, family, work. More in “Settings”.

  3. Scenarios — ready-made answers: paycheck to paycheck, freelancing, credit cards, a trip with friends.
