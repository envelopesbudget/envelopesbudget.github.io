Widgets and shortcuts

Buttons for recording a purchase right on your phone's home screen: voice, a new transaction, the receipt scanner. One tap and you're in the form — without opening the app or scrolling to the “+”.

phone home screen — “Widgets” → “My Budget”; shortcuts — long-press the app icon

Why

Purchases get recorded while you remember them: at the checkout, on your way out of the store. Every extra tap is an excuse to put it off “until tonight” and forget. A widget on the home screen opens what you need right away: voice, the form, or the camera for a receipt.

Five widgets

WidgetSizeWhat it does
“Voice”1 cellA green button with a microphone — voice input.
“Transaction”1 cellA button with a “+” in the color of “+ Transaction” — a new transaction.
“Receipt”1 cellA purple button with a QR code — the receipt scanner.
“Buttons in a column”1 × 2 cellsAll three buttons stacked.
“Buttons in a row”2 × 1 cellsAll three buttons side by side.

The colors match the buttons in the app: green for voice, purple for receipts.

The receipt button isn't for everyone

Everyone gets the separate “Receipt” widget. But in the three-button widgets, the receipt button shows only if the app knows your country's receipts — by the same rule as the button in the app. The rule is in the article “Receipt scanner”. Otherwise the widget has two buttons.

How to add a widget

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on the home screen.

  2. Choose “Widgets”.

  3. Find “My Budget” and the widget you want.

  4. Drag it onto the home screen.

The menu names depend on the phone: some have “Widgets” in the home screen menu, others in a separate list. The “Voice”, “Transaction” and “Receipt” widgets take up one cell, like an app icon.

Shortcuts

A shortcut is the same action as a regular icon: the launcher draws it like app icons, and it lines up with them on any phone.

  1. Press and hold the “My Budget” icon.

  2. “Voice” and “Transaction” appear — tap one to open it right away.

  3. Or drag a shortcut onto the home screen — it stays there as an icon.

There's no shortcut for receipts: a shortcut can't be hidden where the app doesn't know the country's receipts. For receipts, use the “Receipt” widget.

What opens

You tapWhat happens
“Voice”The app opens on “Overview” with the voice input sheet on top.
“Transaction”“Overview”, with the new transaction form on top.
“Receipt”“Overview”, with the scanner camera on top.

After saving, you stay on “Overview” and see the purchase has made it into the budget.

If a PIN is set

The app first asks for your PIN or fingerprint, and right after that opens what you tapped. You don't need to tap anything again.
