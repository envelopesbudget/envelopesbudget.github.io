You give every unit of money a job at the start of the month, then spend from
a specific envelope instead of a vague total.

**No account, no server, no ads.** Your data lives in a database on your
phone. Synchronisation is optional and goes straight into *your* Google
Drive, Dropbox or WebDAV server — the developer has no access to it and no
technical means of seeing it.

## What it does

- **Monthly budget** — plan, actual and what is left, with overspending shown
  before it becomes a surprise.
- **Category goals** — a monthly amount, saving up to a date, or keeping a
  balance; free money is distributed across goals in one tap.
- **Accounts** — cash, bank, credit cards, loans and off-budget savings, each
  with its own currency and decimals, including crypto.
- **Credit cards by the envelope rules** — a purchase reserves money for the
  payment right away, so the card never becomes a surprise at the end of the
  month.
- **Reports** — spending trend, income breakdown, income vs expenses.
- **Receipts, voice entry, scheduled payments, biometric lock.**
- **Twelve interface languages**, with amounts and dates following your own
  format.

## Your data stays yours

Everything the app sends to the cloud can be encrypted with a password you
set — backups, receipt photos and the sync file alike. The password never
leaves your device. The encrypted format is documented in the privacy policy,
so the files can be opened without this app, and the app itself can hand you
a plain, readable copy at any time.
