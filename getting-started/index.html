First steps.

Use the same steps as the app’s built-in welcome guide. Start with today’s balances, then make a plan for the money you already have.

01 Currency and name02 Where your money is03 Your categories04 Assign your money05 Add your first transaction06 A copy off the phone
01

Currency and name

We filled in the currency from your country — check it. But give the budget a name of your own: that makes it easy to recognize on any of your devices.

02

Where your money is

Add every place your money is kept: cash in your wallet and cards at every bank — with today’s balance. Skip even one, and the budget won’t match what you actually have.

03

Your categories

Categories are what your money goes to. Mark what's in your life and adjust the list to suit you: rename, remove what you don't need, add your own.

04

Assign your money

Decide how much of this money goes to each category this month: tap the amount next to a category. Start with the essentials — the rest can be assigned later on the «Budget» screen.

The main rule: assign money to categories until «unassigned» reaches zero. Then every amount in $ has a job, and you can see what is safe to spend.

Try the envelope method ↗
05

Add your first transaction

A transaction is one purchase or one income. Add your first transaction. After that, the app shows where your money goes.

06

A copy off the phone

Phones get lost and break. A cloud keeps a copy of the budget and syncs it with your other devices.

Manual and automatic copies to the device, to a file, or to the cloud. Cloud copies can be protected with a password (AES-GCM).

Start with the money you have. Build a budget that feels like you.
